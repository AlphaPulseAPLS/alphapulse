How the sale works

Fixed rules, enforced by the contract — there is no admin function that can change any of them after deployment.

01

One price, hard caps

1 share = 1 USDT = 10,000 APLS. Maximum 10 shares per wallet, 10,000 shares total. No private rounds, no team allocation, no discounts.

02

70 / 30 split, on-chain

70% of every payment stays in the sale contract as the LP reserve; 30% goes to the treasury. At pool open the reserve seeds the PancakeSwap pool and the LP tokens are burned.

03

Locked until the pool opens

APLS is minted at purchase but stays locked until the pool opens at sellout or after the Nov 12 deadline. 0% tax — buys, sells and transfers are never skimmed.

# AlphaPulse (APLS)

AlphaPulse is a presale dApp built on **BNB Chain** (BNB Smart Chain, BSC).

1 share = 1 USDT = 10,000 APLS, plus a one-time bonus of 1,000 APLS on the first purchase. Maximum 10 shares per wallet. 70% of payments go to the liquidity reserve (LP tokens burned when the pool opens) and 30% to the project treasury. Both contracts are verified on BscScan (Sourcify exact match), the sale contract is ownerless, and transfers unlock when the liquidity pool opens after the presale deadline (Nov 12, 2026, 01:18 AM UTC).
